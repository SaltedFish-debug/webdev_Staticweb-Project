Order Process

The documented flow every order follows, from first contact to handover.

1

Receive Order

Customer provides name, contact number, email, Messenger, quantity, layout, and date needed.

2

Record Order

Staff logs the order into the system instead of a manual Excel file.

3

Down Payment & Agreement

New customers pay 50% down (based on quantity) and sign the agreement. Non-refundable once made.

4

Design Approval

Customer approves the final layout. No approval, no production.

5

Production

Items are printed or produced according to the approved design.

6

Complete & Notify

Order is complete when the client receives the items with a signed receipt.

Business Rules

The rules the system must always reflect.

Down Payment

  • New customers pay a 50% down payment based on order quantity.
  • A signed agreement is required together with the down payment.
  • Once paid and agreed upon, the down payment is not refundable.

Design & Production

  • Design changes are allowed at least 2 days before production, or while there is no final agreement.
  • If the design is not approved, production will not proceed.

Order Information & Completion

  • Customers provide: name, contact number, email, Messenger, number of orders, design/layout, and date needed.
  • An order is completed when the client receives the items with a receipt containing the client's signature.
